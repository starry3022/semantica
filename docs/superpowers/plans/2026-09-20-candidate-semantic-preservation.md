**Goal:** Retain source-supported obligations, conditions, modality, timing and evidence from LLM extraction through RDF, ontology generation and offline replay without asserting conditional statements as unconditional facts.

**Architecture:** Candidate bundle v2 uses named RDF statement resources for all candidate relationships. The native RDFExporter remains the vocabulary/IRI authority and an internal business projection; it does not become the authoritative policy RDF. The LLM receives complete candidate context, while the business ontology excludes transport vocabulary. Existing v1 bundles retain their exact prompt/replay and Explorer projection contract.

**Constraints:** Keep the LLM responsible for business types and predicates. No procurement thresholds or role names in runtime logic. No new dependencies. Work in the user-selected main checkout on feat/opt-0917. Existing untracked agent/state files and old run artifacts are outside the change. Source evidence and candidate/unreviewed status remain explicit. Same-source preservation and schema checks must not be described as independent business approval.
